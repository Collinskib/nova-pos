the idea
Mauzo POS

Your till shouldn't live in someone else's cloud.

LOCAL-FIRST
Everything lives on your machine

One database file in your shop — sales, stock, users. Back it up by copying a file.

OFFLINE-OK
Full checkout, zero bars

Scan, charge, print the receipt — the whole flow works with no internet at all.

YOUR HARDWARE
Runs on the laptop you already own

One command, one store. No terminals to lease, no data leaving the building.

YOUR RULES
Cashiers get the till. You get the keys.

Admin and cashier roles, admin-approved voids and password resets, an audit log on everything.
