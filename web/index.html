FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The desktop till works fully offline — sales, stock, receipts. The web till caches itself and queues sales when the signal drops, then sends them the moment you're back.

Does it take M-Pesa?

You record how each customer paid — cash, M-Pesa, card or credit — and reports split the day by method. Automatic STK push is on the roadmap.

Can I bring in the products I already sell?

Yes. Add them one by one in the app, or on the desktop hub import a whole price list from CSV, JSON or Excel — barcodes, prices, stock and all.

Can I choose what each staff member sees?

Yes. Create a login per person with a role — cashier, manager or admin. Only admins void sales, change prices and manage the team.

Can I sell on credit?

Yes. A credit sale asks for the customer's name, so who owes you — and how much — is on record instead of in a notebook.

How many branches does it handle?

As many as you open. Each branch keeps its own stock, sales and devices; you watch all of them from one dashboard and switch between them on the till.
