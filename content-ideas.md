## Rules this list enforces

- **Merge duplicates**: "organise inventory" / "track inventory" / "improve inventory" → one pillar, not three articles
- **Original material is mandatory** — no article ships on generated text alone
- **Cadence**: 1–2/week max; repurpose each into 2–3 short videos + posts
- **Check codebase before claiming features** — list of confirmed features lives in `README.md`
