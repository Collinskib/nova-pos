businesses in Kenya, with a focus on practical retail operations, sales,

> **Important publishing note:** Treat these as content ideas, not

> capability being discussed. Be especially precise about M-Pesa,

## Recommended content mix

Use this as an initial guideline, not a rigid rule.

-   **35% educational:** Help business owners solve real problems.
-   **25% product demonstrations:** Show how ShujaaPOS works.
-   **15% industry-specific:** Supermarkets, dukas, pharmacies,
    restaurants and wholesalers.
-   **10% founder and behind-the-scenes:** Build familiarity and trust.
-   **10% customer proof:** Testimonials, case studies and real
    outcomes.
-   **5% direct promotion:** Demo invitations, launches and offers.

# 28. Final strategic advice

ShujaaPOS should not publish generic content simply to fill a blog.
Every piece should do at least one of three jobs:

1.  **Attract:** Reach business owners who are searching for answers.
2.  **Educate:** Help them understand how better business systems solve
    real operational problems.
3.  **Convert:** Give interested businesses a reason to book a
    demonstration or speak to your team.

Prioritise useful, original content based on actual Kenyan business
workflows. Use real product screenshots and demonstrations as soon as
they are available. Build trust through transparent claims and real
customer experiences.

**The goal is not to become the POS brand that posts the most. It is to
become the POS brand that consistently gives Kenyan business owners
useful answers --- and gives them a compelling reason to try
ShujaaPOS.**
